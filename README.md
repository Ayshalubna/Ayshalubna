## 🛡️ Flagship project

### [Amanah — Agentic AML & KYC Investigation Copilot (Arabic–English)](https://github.com/Ayshalubna/amanah)
Multi-agent **LangGraph** system that helps bank compliance teams onboard customers and investigate suspicious activity, built around UAE workflows (Emirates ID, UN / local sanctions lists, goAML reporting). The investigator approves every decision.

- **Bilingual sanctions screening:** Arabic normalisation, transliteration and phonetic keys give **0.99 precision and recall** on 1,180 labelled queries, against 0.68 recall for plain fuzzy matching
- **Transaction risk:** LightGBM + SHAP (ROC-AUC 0.97) catches **90%** of suspicious customers vs 70% for rules at similar alert volume
- **Controls:** policy-floor and citation guardrails, human-in-the-loop `interrupt()`, audit trail, 18 tests and an evaluation gate in CI
- Runs free with a local LLM (Ollama) or with no LLM at all
